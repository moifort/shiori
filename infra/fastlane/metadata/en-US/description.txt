Shiori is your library in your pocket.

Take a photo of a book cover: Shiori recognizes it and fills in its record for you — title, author, publisher, summary, genre and series. Novels, graphic novels, manga, comics or audiobooks: everything has its place.

FEATURES
• Cover scan: one photo is all it takes to catalogue a book
• Series, volume by volume: your series grouped together, with the volumes you’re missing and the ones still to come
• New releases: Shiori watches your series and lets you know when the next volume comes out
• Reading tracker: to read, reading, read or dropped, with your star ratings, favorites and notes
• Dashboard: books read per year, pages read and hours listened, favorite genres, to-read pile
• Kindle and Audible: import your existing library and keep it in sync
• Print and digital together: a book you own on paper and on your e-reader stays a single record

WITH FRIENDS
Invite your friends: you each see the other’s library, favorites and current reads. Pick up an idea from their shelves in one tap, and set each other a reading challenge for the year. Your notes stay private, and a hidden book is never shared.

SHIORI PREMIUM
Shiori is free, with scans included every month. Premium unlocks unlimited scans, scanning a whole shelf in one photo, and the complete catalogue of your series, upcoming volumes included. Choose a monthly or a yearly plan; the yearly plan starts with a free month.

Your subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, from your App Store account settings.
